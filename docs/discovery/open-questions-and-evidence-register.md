## How to interpret this register

- **Stakeholder finding** records what interviewees reported or preferred. It is not automatically a verified legal fact, technical proof, or signed policy.
- **Reference corroboration** means an authoritative or relevant source was located. It does not mean counsel or the responsible professional has approved the system interpretation.
- **Decision recommendation** is the proposed product/architecture direction, subject to the named review.
- **Evidence status** is deliberately separate from the stakeholder answer. “Pending” and “not supplied” are not confirmations.
- Do not store confidential client records, credentials, tax matrices containing confidential material, or personal data in this repository. Link to a controlled evidence location and record its custodian/version instead.

## Executive assessment

The interview findings provide useful direction and resolve several product-level preferences: manual tax preparation/export first; visible conflict handling; bounded online revalidation for sensitive offline actions; a controlled pilot before scaling; English-first UI with Amharic document support; and separate firm/individual subscription entitlements.

They do **not** close Phase 0. The most important corrections and conditions are:

1. **Windows 10 production support:** standard Windows 10 support ended on 2025-10-14. Do not promise secure, supported production use on ordinary unsupported Windows 10. Make a currently supported Windows release the production baseline. Windows 10 compatibility may be retained only for a specifically identified edition/device cohort with verified active ESU/support coverage, documented risk acceptance and a sunset date.
2. **Accounting frameworks:** add IPSAS where applicable to charities and societies and require entity classification plus the current AABE-adopted/adapted/amended framework for each engagement. Do not hard-code Q3 as an exhaustive list.
3. **Tax matrix:** Q4 is a stakeholder assertion, not yet an auditable verified control. Obtain the actual controlled matrix and reconcile it against current federal, regional and city instruments, including amendments, commencement dates, transition rules and superseded provisions.
4. **Privacy/data governance:** Proclamation 1321/2024 is identified and listed as in force, but counsel must still map obligations, controller/processor roles by purpose, registration, cross-border processing, data-subject rights, security, incident response and contracts.
5. **Professional licensing:** verify current AABE individual and firm licensing categories, scope, conditions, status and signing authority before enabling regulated services or report sign-off.
6. **Retention:** implement record-class retention and legal holds. Commercial Code Article 66 states ten years for books of account and accounting documents; Tax Administration Proclamation 983/2016 contains a longer-of-period rule and proceeding-related extension. Counsel must validate scope and any later amendments before implementation.
7. **Offline/sync:** “show every conflict” is not a complete conflict policy. Never silently merge or overwrite financial postings, audit evidence, approvals, tax outputs or signed reports. Use entity-specific deterministic merge rules for low-risk metadata and authorized human resolution for material conflicts.
8. **Scale/operations:** “large-scale” and “moderate budget” need measurable pilot, concurrency, storage, sync-burst, availability, RPO/RTO and restore targets before architecture sizing or production deployment.

## New cross-cutting risks and decisions

### R-001 — Windows 10 lifecycle (blocks supported production scope)
Microsoft states Windows 10 reached end of support on 2025-10-14; ordinary installations no longer receive security updates. Compatibility testing may continue, but do not represent unsupported Windows 10 as a secure production platform. Confirm exact SKU, version and ESU coverage if an exception is proposed. Reference: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/deployment/updates-lifecycle/windows-10-support-has-ended-on-october-14-2025

### R-002 — Applicable accounting standards (blocks framework catalogue)
AABE's published standards page lists IFRS, IFRS for SMEs and IPSAS applicable to charities and societies, and says ISA applies as adopted/adapted/amended by the Board. Build framework selection around entity classification and engagement period. Reference: https://aabe.gov.et/accounting-and-auditing-standards-2/

### R-003 — Tax matrix not yet inspected (blocks tax-rule approval)
Ministry of Justice's tax sector index lists current amendments and regulations. At this review date it includes Regulation 597/2026 (dated 2026-11-06) and VAT Regulation 570/2025. Reconcile the specialist matrix to official texts and regional/city authority rules before approval. References:
- https://justice.gov.et/en/sector/taxation-customs/
- https://justice.gov.et/en/law/570-2025-value-added-tax-regulation/
- https://justice.gov.et/en/law/ (search for Regulation 597/2026)

### R-004 — Retention and legal hold (blocks deletion/retention implementation)
Commercial Code Proclamation 1243/2021 Article 66 states ten years for books of accounts and accounting documents. Tax Administration Proclamation 983/2016 Article 17 requires retention for the longer of the Commercial Code period or five years from filing, with extension where proceedings remain open. Counsel must confirm amendments, scope and interaction with professional rules. References:
- https://ethiodata.et/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Commercial-Code-of-Ethiopia-English-Proclamation-No.-1243_2021.pdf
- https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/%E1%8A%A0%E1%8B%8B%E1%8C%85-%E1%89%81%E1%8C%A5%E1%88%AD-983-2008-%E1%8A%A5%E1%8A%95%E1%8C%8D%E1%88%8A%E1%8B%98%E1%8A%9B.pdf

### R-005 — Personal data obligations (blocks real client data)
Ministry of Justice lists Personal Data Protection Proclamation 1321/2024 as in force, dated 2024-07-24. Counsel must confirm implementing rules, registration, role allocation, transfer requirements and any subsequent instruments. Reference: https://justice.gov.et/en/law/personal-data-protection-proclamation/

### R-006 — Professional licensing and signing authority (blocks regulated workflow enablement)
AABE published Directive No. 1096/2025 on licensing accounting and auditing professionals and registration of professional accountancy bodies. Extract the applicable licence categories and permitted services from the full directive and obtain professional/legal review. Reference: https://aabe.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Directive-No.-1096.2025-English-Version_organized1.pdf

## Required evidence package before changing any item to verified

1. Controlled source copy or official URL, issuer, exact title/number, publication and commencement dates, relevant clauses, access date and supersession check.
2. Stakeholder evidence: interview date, role (not unnecessary personal data), exact answer, scope and any conditions.
3. Responsible reviewer, decision, review date and written rationale.
4. Technical proof where applicable: threat tests, test fixtures, benchmarks, restore evidence, signed-off prototype report or integration authorization.
5. Traceability from requirement to design, implementation, test and operational owner.
6. Explicit unresolved assumptions and risk owner. Never silently convert missing evidence to “verified”.
